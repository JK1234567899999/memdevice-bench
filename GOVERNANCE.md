# Governance

MemDeviceBench uses a lightweight maintainer model during the alpha stage.

## Roles

- **Contributor:** anyone with an accepted contribution.
- **Reviewer:** a contributor trusted to review a defined technical area.
- **Maintainer:** responsible for releases, security response, roadmap, and final merge decisions.

## Decision process

Routine decisions use lazy consensus in public issues or pull requests. Changes to the data schema, metric definitions, licensing, governance, or backwards compatibility require a written design issue and approval by at least two maintainers or, while only one maintainer exists, one maintainer plus one independent domain reviewer.

## Becoming a reviewer or maintainer

Selection is based on sustained technical contributions, constructive review, scientific rigor, and reliability. Contributor counts are never a reason by themselves to grant project roles.

## Conflict of interest

Reviewers should disclose competing commercial, publication, or collaboration interests when they could affect a technical decision.
